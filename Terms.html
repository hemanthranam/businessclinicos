Terms of Service & Operational Policies

Effective Date: June 2026

1. Platform Overview

Business Clinic OS Platform ("The System") links patients, doctors, and decentralized clinic operations into an automated SaaS enterprise pipeline.

2. HIPAA Security & Data Disclosures

All client and patient data transmitted via the website frontend or internal dashboards is encrypted end-to-end and stored securely inside HIPAA-compliant Google Sheets database arrays. Data is never sold or handed over to unauthorized third-party services.

3. Acceptable Use

Authorized clinic staff, doctors, and system administrators accessing the dashboard suite must use secure enterprise Google SSO accounts. Tampering with internal booking metrics, records, or logs will trigger audit procedures and immediate session termination.
