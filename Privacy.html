Digital Privacy Policy

Information Logged

We automatically log entry timestamps, network IP addresses, and generalized geolocation information when lead forms or employee modifications are committed to our secure cloud database.

Securing Intake Streams

Your "Reason for Visit" symptoms, contact details, and dates scheduled are treated with strict confidentiality. Operations logs allow clinic directors to trace modifications safely via internal audit logs.

SMS and Notification Data

We collect and use phone numbers strictly to process appointment bookings and send automated confirmation messages via WhatsApp or SMS. We do not share this data with third parties.
